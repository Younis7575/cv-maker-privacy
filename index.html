CV Maker Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how CV Maker collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our mobile application.

By using CV Maker, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

CV Maker allows users to create professional CVs by entering personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • Education Details
  • Work Experience
  • Skills
  • Projects
  • Achievements

This information is stored locally on your device.

2. Local Data Storage

All CV data is stored locally on your device using secure storage.

We do not upload your personal data to our servers.

3. PDF Generation

CV Maker generates PDF files based on user-entered information.

PDF files are stored on your device only.

4. Cloud Storage

CV Maker may use cloud storage services to store templates and images.

These files do not contain personal user information.

5. Advertisements

CV Maker uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

AdMob may collect:

  • Device Information
  • Advertising ID
  • Usage Data

Google Privacy Policy:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

6. Permissions Used

Storage Permission

Used to save CV PDF files on your device.

Internet Permission

Used to load templates and show advertisements.

7. Payments

CV Maker may include premium templates.

Payments are processed securely by Google Play.

We do not store payment information.

8. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your data.

However, no system is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

CV Maker does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years old.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Changes will be reflected on this page.
